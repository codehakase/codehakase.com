# Francis Sunday
> Software engineer at Talstack, leading delivery of its HRIS product in Haskell and PostgreSQL. Previously worked on cross-border payments in Go at Nala.

## About
Francis Sunday (@codehakase) is a software engineer based in Lagos, Nigeria, with experience across product engineering, backend systems, and payments infrastructure. Francis is particularly interested in distributed systems, developer tools, and keeping software reliable in production.

At [Talstack](https://talstack.com), a people management platform, Francis is a software engineer leading delivery of the HRIS product. The product covers employee records, documents, access controls, and a workflows engine. Most of this work is in Haskell and PostgreSQL.

Previously, Francis worked on cross-border payments at [Nala](https://nala.com), building backend systems in Go. There, Francis contributed to the [Rafiki API](https://rafiki.com), covering remittances from Europe to Africa, payout integrations across African regions, and internal tools including a real-time transaction monitoring dashboard.

Francis also runs [Hakase Development Studio](https://hakasestudio.com), a small studio for backend engineering and infrastructure.
